A weekly roundup documenting the massive Spring release cycle following a May delay caused by AI-driven CVEs, urging an immediate upgrade to Spring Boot 4.1. It catalogs dozens of simultaneous releases including Spring AI 2.0 GA, Spring Cloud, Spring Data 2026.0.0, Spring Security CVE fixes, and updates to Spring Batch, Kafka, and AMQP, with highlights on tool calling in Spring AI 2.0 and semantic caching from community contributor Craig Walls.
